# Fluent Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026

This policy explains what the Fluent app ("Fluent", "we", "us") collects, why,
who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Fluent is operated by
Talha Hussain, Pakistan. Contact: **talhatechwith@gmail.com**.

## 1. What we collect

**Your account**
- When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, email address
  and a unique account ID. With Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your
  email; we then receive a private relay address from Apple.

**Your learning profile and progress**
- The answers you give when setting up Fluent:
  - the language and accent you are learning, and your level;
  - your goals, preferred speaking style and daily goal;
  - your coach's name.
- Your progress: lessons completed, practice time, XP, streaks, scores, the
  words and sounds you find hard, and the topics of your recent lessons.

**Your voice**
- While you read a lesson aloud, audio from your microphone is streamed to
  Microsoft Azure Speech so your pronunciation can be scored. We do not store
  this audio on our servers.
- Short clips of you saying words you found hard are saved **only on your
  phone**, so you can compare your voice with the correct pronunciation. They
  are never uploaded.

**Usage and diagnostics**
- App usage events, such as sign-in method, onboarding steps, lessons started
  and completed, scores and paywall views. They are collected through Google
  Firebase Analytics.
- Crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics. These include device type,
  operating system version and your account ID, so crashes can be fixed.

**Purchases**
- If you subscribe, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We do
  not receive your card details. We receive your subscription status.
  Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, which receives your account ID
  and purchase records.

We do not collect your location, contacts or photos, and we do not use your
data for advertising.

## 2. How we use it

- To run the app: sign you in, keep your progress across devices, and generate
  and score your lessons.
- To personalise lessons: your level, goals and hard words shape each new
  paragraph.
- To keep the app working and improve it: crash reports and aggregate usage.
- To manage your subscription and free trial.

## 3. Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run the app's features:

| Provider | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics) | Account details, profile, progress, usage and crash data | Sign-in, storage and sync, analytics, crash reporting |
| Apple / Google sign-in | Sign-in request | Signing you in |
| Microsoft Azure Speech | Your voice while you read, and the lesson text | Scoring your pronunciation |
| Supabase | Requests from the app (with your account ID) | Running our server functions |
| Groq (AI model provider) | Your language, level, goals, words to practise, recent lesson topics, and sounds measured in your reading | Writing lessons and pronunciation respellings. It does not receive your name or email. |
| Apple App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat | Purchase records | Subscriptions |

We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. Voice clips stay on
your phone until you delete the app or your account. Analytics and crash data
are kept according to Firebase's retention settings.

## 5. Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** at any time in the app: **Settings → Delete
  account**. This permanently deletes your profile, progress and sign-in from
  our systems, and the voice clips on your phone.
- **Microphone:** you can turn off microphone access in your phone's settings.
  Lessons need it to score you.
- Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR in the EU/UK or CCPA in
  California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete
  your data, or to object to its use. Email **talhatechwith@gmail.com** and we will
  respond within 30 days.

## 6. Children

Fluent is not intended for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU/UK). We do
not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us data,
contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with Google
Firebase and Supabase, which use industry-standard security. No system is
perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the
United States. They are bound by safeguards such as standard contractual
clauses.

## 9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the effective date above and, for
significant changes, tell you in the app.
